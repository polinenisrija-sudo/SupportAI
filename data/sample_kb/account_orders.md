# Account and Orders Help

## Changing or Cancelling an Order
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that, the order enters fulfillment and can no longer be modified. If your order has already shipped, you can refuse delivery or start a return once it arrives.

## Resetting Your Password
Select Forgot Password on the login page and enter your email. A reset link is sent immediately and expires after 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept personal checks, cash on delivery, or cryptocurrency. Your card is charged when your order ships, not when it is placed.

## Promo Codes and Discounts
Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with gift cards for partial discounts and do not apply to clearance items. Students and military members receive 10% off with verified ID through our partner portal.

## Contacting Support
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Mountain Time. You can reach us at support@northwind.example or by chat. Average email response time is under 24 hours.
