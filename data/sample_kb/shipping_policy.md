# Northwind Outfitters Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping Options
We ship all orders within the United States from our warehouse in Denver, Colorado. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $5.95. Expedited shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.95. Overnight shipping is delivered the next business day and costs $29.95 for orders placed before 1 PM Mountain Time.

## Free Shipping
Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping within the contiguous United States. Free shipping does not apply to Alaska, Hawaii, or oversized items such as kayaks and tents larger than 6 person capacity.

## Order Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day. You will receive an email with a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

## International Shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia. International orders take 7-14 business days and shipping is a flat $24.95. Customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees charged by their country.

## Tracking and Lost Packages
You can track your package with the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email. If a tracked package shows no movement for 7 days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier. Lost packages are replaced or refunded in full.
