# Returns and Refunds

## 30-Day Return Window
You may return most new, unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with all tags attached. Return shipping is free for defective items; for all other returns a $6.50 return label fee is deducted from your refund.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned
For hygiene and safety reasons, the following cannot be returned: used sleeping bags, opened water filters, base layers worn outside of try-on, and gift cards. Final-sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or exchange.

## How to Start a Return
Log in to your account, open Order History, and select Start a Return next to the order. Print the prepaid label and drop the package at any UPS location. Returns without a return authorization number will be delayed.

## Refund Timing
Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive your return. Your bank may take an additional 3-5 business days to post the credit. Shipping fees paid at checkout are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

## Exchanges
We offer free exchanges for a different size or color within 30 days, subject to availability. If the item is out of stock, we will refund you instead.
