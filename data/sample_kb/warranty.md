# Warranty Information

## Limited Lifetime Warranty on Backpacks
Northwind backpacks and duffel bags are covered by a limited lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. This covers broken zippers, torn seams, and failed buckles. It does not cover normal wear and tear, fading, or damage from misuse, accidents, or modification.

## Tents and Shelters
Tents carry a 2-year warranty covering waterproofing failure, pole breakage under normal use, and seam failure. Damage caused by wind when stakes were not used, or from fire, is not covered.

## Footwear Warranty
Boots and trail shoes are covered for 12 months from the purchase date against sole separation and stitching defects. Worn tread is not considered a defect.

## How to File a Warranty Claim
Email warranty@northwind.example with your order number and photos of the damage. We respond within 2 business days. Approved claims are repaired, replaced, or credited at our discretion. You do not need to pay for shipping on approved warranty claims.
